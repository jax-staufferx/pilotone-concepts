Pilot One: design options

Same words and photos in every version. Light and dark mode in each (toggle top right).

OrigOriginal demoThe page as it was. ARefinedNo dotted grid, flat paper instead of blurred shadows, solid headings with blue only on key words, scales to big screens. BSharpA with Archivo headings, caps buttons, and sections opening on a dashed flight-route line with their name. CLayeredA with stacked paper on the key pieces: the hero photo as a framed print, services as panels, contact details on a sheet. DGlobe (more flair)Built from the visitor's side: two clear doors (selling / buying), a live 3D globe of trade routes flowing into the bureau, and a scroll-or-click "how the bureau works" in three steps. EDepartures (more flair)The name taken literally: numbers and brands flip in on a departures board, the offers are tickets, the statement is a route a parcel travels as you scroll, contact is a boarding pass, and a flight path marks where you are. FFinal (D + flowing logos + categories + partner)The version Pilot One asked for: D's globe with routes into Lithuania, the flowing logo rows, "What we offer" with four categories that each download their brand list plus the full list, a "Become a partner" registration for price list access, and contact below.
